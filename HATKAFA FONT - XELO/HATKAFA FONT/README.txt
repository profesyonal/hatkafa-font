HATKAFA HEBREW TYPEFACE
===========================================================================

Hatkafa is a custom Hebrew display typeface with a modern experimental 
modular character. Built from structured geometric forms, its bold and 
distinctive appearance creates a visual language associated with strength, 
discipline, military aesthetics, and messages of peace. Designed with careful 
attention to detail, it is fully optimized for OpenType features, including 
right-to-left (RTL) text directionality and precise diacritic (Nikud) placement. 

This font project was custom commissioned by Rebecca K and prepared for 
release under the SIL Open Font License.
